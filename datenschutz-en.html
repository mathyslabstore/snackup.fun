2. This website (snackup.fun)

This website does not use cookies or any tracking or analytics tools. No external service (fonts, maps, videos) is embedded that would transfer data to third parties — the font used is hosted locally on this server.

When you visit this website, our hosting provider automatically collects technical access data (server log files) transmitted by your browser, e.g. IP address, date and time of the request, page accessed, browser and operating system used. This processing serves to ensure the smooth operation and security of our technical systems (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and is automatically deleted after a short period.

If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide in order to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

3. The SnackUp app

SnackUp works entirely locally ("local-first"): there is no server to which the app transmits data. All your information (profile, workout history, settings) is stored exclusively on your device. As the provider, we have no access to this data.

Depending on the features used, the app requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications — for local reminders; scheduling happens entirely on the device, nothing is sent to a server.
  • Location (optional) — only if you enable the recurring-places feature; processing happens locally on the device and is never transmitted or sent to us.

Since all data is stored locally, you can delete or export it yourself at any time — directly within the app (export feature) or by uninstalling the app.
